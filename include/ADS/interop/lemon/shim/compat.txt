# std::allocator that C++20 removed, so that they compile in C++20, in the
# format of rewrites.txt: they are applied only where a program with a map
# of LEMON does not compile as it is [see ADSLemon.cmake and
# extlib/makefile-libLEMON], and abLEMON needs none of them.
